# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Welcome contributors of every background
and experience level. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Respect privacy,
consent and intellectual property.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are
not welcome. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict access
when necessary to keep collaboration safe and productive.

Report concerns privately through a maintainer's published contact. Maintainers
should handle reports discreetly and fairly, and avoid unnecessary disclosure.
